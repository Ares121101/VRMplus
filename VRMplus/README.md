\# VRMplus 



A Chrome browser extension that enhances the Victron Energy VRM Portal with fast chart navigation and real-time battery capacity telemetry.



\## Features



\- \*\*Quick Chart Zoom:\*\* Left-click on Highcharts tooltips to immediately zoom in on specific time ranges. Right-click anywhere on a chart to step backward through your view history.

\- \*\*Battery Time-to-Go Widget:\*\* Calculates real-time time remaining to empty (or to 100% charge) based on rolling power averages to prevent jumpy numbers.

\- \*\*Live Energy Tracking:\*\* Displays current stored energy vs. total capacity in kWh directly on your dashboard (`stored kWh / total kWh`).

\- \*\*Fully Configurable:\*\* Easily tweak battery capacity (Ah), system voltage (V), minimum SOC limit, and buffer smoothing window directly from the widget settings panel.



\## Installation



1\. Download or clone this repository to your local machine.

2\. Open Chrome and navigate to `chrome://extensions/`.

3\. Enable \*\*Developer mode\*\* using the toggle in the top-right corner.

4\. Click \*\*Load unpacked\*\* and select the folder containing these files.

5\. Open your Victron VRM Dashboard (`https://vrm.victronenergy.com/`).
